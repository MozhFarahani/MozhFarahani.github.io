A multi-agency effort with the Bureau of Reclamation and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. I work on the calibration workstream, where I built the end-to-end HPC pipeline that uses the trained emulators to generate parameters for ~97,000 HUC12 subbasins across the contiguous U.S., runs large simulation ensembles, and automates parameter selection gauge by gauge through the river network. I also help coordinate region-by-region delivery to partners.

For the project team, I built a Python and Plotly tool that turns HPC model output into interactive diagnostic dashboards, with CAMELS-X benchmarking, streamflow signatures, and automated failure flags, to find and diagnose poorly performing gauges.
