The WANA Affinity Group is a grassroots community, started in 2023, that connects UCAR, NSF NCAR, and UCAR Community Programs (UCP) scientists who work in or have ties to the West Asia–North Africa region, where extreme heat, water scarcity, flash floods, and dust storms are intensifying. With a UCAR President’s Strategic Initiative Fund award, our team interviewed researchers and surveyed staff to build an open inventory of past and current WANA projects, covering cloud seeding, renewable-energy forecasting, dust and air quality, hydrology, S2S prediction, and public health.

The final report summarizes lessons learned on data sharing, realistic scoping, and building trust with partners, and identifies future priorities and funding paths. We presented the findings at a CGD seminar in August 2026.
