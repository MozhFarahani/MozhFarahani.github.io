I am a scientist at the National Center for Atmospheric Research with a Ph.D. and 7 years of experience combining machine learning with physics-based models of land, water, and climate. My work runs from research to reliable code: surrogate modeling and optimization, federated learning, causal inference, and uncertainty quantification, built into end-to-end pipelines for training, inference, and evaluation on high-performance computing. My methods are now used in multi-agency projects, and I work closely with domain experts and partner teams, explaining results clearly to technical and non-technical audiences. Looking ahead, I am most interested in physics-informed and hybrid models, where machine learning and physics-based models learn together, and in using information theory to understand what these models learn and why.
